## 🌍 The Veribase Mission

**83% of Kenya's workforce** operates in the informal economy — hawkers, freelancers, fundis, small traders, boda boda drivers. These people have:

- ✅ Years of experience
- ✅ Loyal customers
- ✅ Real skills and track records

But they cannot prove any of it to new customers, lenders, or employers. There's no LinkedIn for a jua kali mechanic. No verified review system for a mama mboga. No credit history for a first-time borrower with 10 years of on-time mobile money payments.

**Veribase fills this gap.**

## 🔑 Landing Page Sections

### Hero
- Clear value proposition: *"Africa's economy, made legible"*
- CTA: Get Verified / Verify Someone
- Trust badge showcase

### How It Works
1. Register on Veribase
2. Submit your documents and work history
3. Get your Trust Score and Verification Badge
4. Share your badge everywhere

### Use Cases
- **Hire a fundi** → Verify their identity and past work
- **Apply for a loan** → Prove your payment history
- **Find a freelancer** → Check their verified portfolio
- **Get a job** → Show your Veribase Trust Score

### Pricing
- Individual plans from KES 99
- Business verification from KES 499/month
- Enterprise custom plans

### Legal Pages
- Full privacy policy aligned with Kenya Data Protection Act (2019)
- Terms of service
- Data Processing Agreement for B2B clients
