## 🎯 What is Onyx Digital?

Onyx Digital is a social media growth agency platform that helps businesses and creators grow their online presence without the overhead of a traditional marketing team.

**The core insight:** Most businesses already have content — they just don't have the time, skills, or budget to optimise it. Onyx Digital uses AI to:

1. **Rewrite and enhance** existing captions for maximum engagement
2. **Schedule content** at peak times for each platform
3. **Run paid ads** on top of organic posts for amplified reach
4. **Track performance** and iterate automatically

---

## ✨ Platform Features

### 🤖 AI Caption Engine
- Paste any caption → get 5 AI-optimised variations
- Platform-specific tone (Instagram vs LinkedIn vs TikTok)
- Hashtag recommendations based on niche + trend analysis
- CTA (call to action) injection
- Emoji strategy built in

### 📅 Content Scheduling
- Connect Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X, LinkedIn
- Smart scheduling at peak engagement times per platform
- Bulk upload and queue management
- Repeat post recycling for evergreen content

### 📊 Paid Ads Management
- Facebook & Instagram Ads integration
- Budget allocation across posts
- Audience targeting by interest, location, and demographics
- Performance tracking and ROI reporting

### 📈 Analytics Dashboard
- Engagement rate per post
- Follower growth tracking
- Best-performing content identification
- Weekly performance reports
